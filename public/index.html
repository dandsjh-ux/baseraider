❓ How to Play

Goal

Have the most points when the last raid ends. Every word card in your house = 10 pts. Every raider you knock out = 2 pts.

Phases

🛠️ Prep: other bases are locked. Build walls with your blocks and buy defences.
⚔️ Raid: sneak into other houses, touch word cards to grab them, and run home to bank them!

Roles

🛡️ Defending (inside your compound): you hold a stick — click to whack raiders back.
🏃 Raiding (anywhere else): no weapon, but you run much faster. Each word you carry slows you a little.

At 0 HP you're knocked out, drop any words you were carrying, and respawn at home.
